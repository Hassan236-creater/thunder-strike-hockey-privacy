## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Thunder Strike Hockey**! This Privacy Policy explains how **STEPHANI BARNES, L.L.C.** ("we", "our", or "us") handles information in connection with the Thunder Strike Hockey mobile application for Android.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Thunder Strike Hockey is designed as a standalone, offline arcade sports game. **We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, device identifiers, or sensitive information from our users.**

---

## 2. Information Collection and Use

### Personal Data
We do **not** collect any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, telephone number, or payment details). You do not need to register an account, sign in, or provide any credentials to enjoy the game.

### Device and Hardware Data
Thunder Strike Hockey does **not** collect hardware serial numbers, IMEI, MAC addresses, advertising IDs (AAID/IDFA), or phone state data.

### Location Data
We do **not** request, track, or access your precise or approximate GPS location.

### Diagnostic & Usage Analytics
The application does **not** integrate third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics for Firebase, Flurry, or Unity Analytics tracking). We do not record your in-game behavior, session durations, or gameplay telemetry.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

- **Zero Advertising:** Thunder Strike Hockey contains no third-party banner ads, interstitial ads, rewarded video ads, or ad networks.
- **Zero Third-Party SDKs:** The game operates without external tracking libraries, social network plugins, or third-party marketing SDKs.
- **No Data Sharing or Sale:** Because no data is collected, we never sell, trade, license, or share user data with any third parties.

---

## 4. Local Data Retention & Offline Architecture

Any gameplay state (such as sound preference toggles, game settings, or local high scores) is stored **exclusively locally on your device** via standard sandboxed local storage (Unity PlayerPrefs). This data never leaves your device and is erased if you uninstall the application or clear app data via Android settings.

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Thunder Strike Hockey is built to be safe and enjoyable for players of all ages, including families and children.
- We do **not** knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA/UK).
- The game contains no chat rooms, forums, user-generated content, or social sharing mechanisms.
- The game complies fully with the Google Play Families Policy, COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act), and GDPR-K.
